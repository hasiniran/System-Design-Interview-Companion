# Playbook 01 Practice

## Exercise 1

Your interviewer says:

> Design Dropbox.

Do not design it.

Spend the first five minutes completing only:

- Functional requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- Clarifying questions

Stop there.

---

## Exercise 2

Estimate the scale.

Assume:

- 50 million registered users
- 5 million daily active users
- Average file size: 5 MB

Estimate:

- Daily uploads
- Daily storage
- Annual storage
- Read/write ratio

---

## Exercise 3

Draw the simplest possible architecture.

Limit yourself to **five boxes**.

No caching.

No messaging.

No CDN.

No microservices.

Just enough to satisfy the requirements.

---

## Reflection

Did you introduce any technology before identifying the problem it solved?

If yes, which one?

Would your architecture still work if the traffic doubled?

Why?
