# How to Use This Workbook

Every worksheet follows the same interview structure.

```
Requirements
↓

Assumptions

↓

Capacity Estimation

↓

High-Level Design

↓

Deep Dive

↓

Bottlenecks

↓

Trade-offs
```

Don't skip sections.

If you're unsure about something, write your assumptions.

Interviewers expect assumptions.

They do not expect mind reading.

---

## Rules

- Think out loud.
- Keep drawings simple.
- Make reasonable assumptions.
- Explain every decision.
- Discuss trade-offs.
- Don't chase the perfect answer.

The objective is to become more structured with every exercise.
